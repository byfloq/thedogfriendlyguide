Event and community terms

The Guide is operated by SECOND NATURE SL. Contact: studio@byfloq.com.

The Guide is for everyone. Organised community events are for dogs up to 7 kg, within the weight ranges stated in each event. The owner is responsible for accurate dog details, appropriate equipment and supervising their dog throughout. Venue rules and access can change; check the current details before leaving.

Events in this release are free. A reservation is confirmed only when the app shows a confirmed ticket. A waiting-list request is not a confirmed reservation. When a place becomes available, the next eligible person receives an offer valid for up to two hours, ending when the event starts if sooner. Please cancel before the event starts if you cannot attend, so another person can join. The host or team may cancel for weather, safety or other practical reasons; affected participants receive an update. There are no charges or refunds in this free release. Paid events require separate published terms before payments are enabled.

The host’s notices are for practical event updates. They are not an open chat. Share only content you own or have permission to use; obtain permission from identifiable people before sharing photos. You can report content, block people, remove your own moments and contact the team. We may hide unsafe or inappropriate content and suspend abusive accounts.

Connecting in Pack requires acceptance by both people. An invitation link should be shared only with intended recipients. Do not publish private invitations or other people’s personal information.

The Guide’s routes, walking times and availability are estimates. Use safe public paths, follow local signs and adapt the outing to your dog and the conditions. Nothing here limits rights that cannot be excluded under applicable consumer law.
