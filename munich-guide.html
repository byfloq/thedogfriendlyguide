Kanso Coffee Lab

Specialty coffee · Altstadt-Lehel

A quiet courtyard coffee lab in the Residenzpassage, with a pared-back interior and a considered approach to espresso and filter coffee. Confirm current dog access directly with the café before visiting. Photography: Kanso Coffee Lab.

Man Versus Machine · Glockenbach

Specialty coffee · Glockenbachviertel

The original Man Versus Machine coffee shop on Müllerstraße, serving specialty espresso, filter coffee, matcha and Franzbrötchen in the heart of Glockenbachviertel. Confirm current dog access directly with the café before visiting. Photography: Man Versus Machine.

Mon–Fri 08:00–19:00 · Sat–Sun 09:00–19:00

The Spirited Goat

Specialty coffee & matcha · Schwabing

A neighbourhood coffee and matcha bar on Hohenzollernstraße, with beans from European roasteries and carefully sourced teas. Stop for an espresso or a slow filter coffee in Schwabing. Confirm current dog access directly with the café before visiting. Photography: The Spirited Goat.

Mon–Fri 07:00–15:30 · Sat 07:30–14:00 · Sun 09:00–13:30
