Privacy notice

The Dog Friendly Guide is operated by SECOND NATURE SL, NIF B-67432732, Avenida de Castelldefels 71, Esc B 1-2, 08860 Castelldefels, Barcelona, Spain. Contact: studio@byfloq.com.

We use your account information, dog profile and preferences to provide the Guide, save your choices and manage your reservations. You can browse places and routes without an account. An account is needed when you save to the cloud, share a moment, connect with someone or reserve an event.

Moments and photos. Your private Pack consists of people you choose to connect with; both people agree to connect. Your moments are visible to that Pack. If “My moments on place pages” is enabled, the team can review and publish your contribution on a place page under your chosen public name. You can turn this off in Privacy and delete your own moments. We remove location metadata from uploaded photos. Only share photos you have permission to share. A separate choice controls whether an event photo can be shown on its club page.

Events. We store the event, your dog’s name, reservation status, ticket code, attendance and your photo choice. The host sees what is needed to welcome and check in participants, including an indication if you do not want to appear in photographs. You can change the default photo choice in Privacy and the choice for each reservation before confirming. After attending, you can choose whether other attendees can find you and invite you to connect. Declined requests remain private.

Reservations in this version are free. Payments and Apple Pay are disabled; we do not collect card details or send payment data to Stripe in this version. If paid events are introduced, their price and applicable terms will be shown before booking and this notice will be updated.

Location and notifications. Location access is optional and requested when needed to suggest a nearby place or guide a route. You can choose a place manually. Photo uploads do not contain exact GPS metadata. Optional nearby reminders can use location only if you enable them. Push tokens are used to deliver notifications you enable and are reassigned when you change accounts on a device. You can turn off event notification types in Settings and device permissions in iOS Settings.

Analytics and diagnostics. Anonymous aggregate screen and reservation counts are collected only after you enable that option; those counts do not contain account identifiers. Essential error diagnostics use Sentry with fixed error categories and technical information, without your photos, names, messages, precise location or authentication tokens. Replay and advertising tracking are disabled.

Providers and purposes. Supabase hosts authentication, the database and private photo storage; Expo/Apple deliver enabled notifications; Sentry processes error diagnostics. The embedded map uses Google Maps. We use these providers to deliver the service and maintain its security, with the applicable safeguards for international transfers. We do not sell personal data. Partner reports are aggregated; figures for fewer than five people are suppressed. Any affiliate relationship is identified where a link is offered.

Legal bases and retention. Account and booking processing is necessary to provide the service you request. Optional public photos, location, notifications and analytics follow your choices and permissions; security and abuse prevention support our legitimate interests. We keep account content while your account is active or until you remove it, subject to narrowly necessary security records. On account deletion we remove private content, connections, future reservations and device tokens; past tickets are anonymised. Residual storage is swept by a retry process. Minimal deletion markers prevent old in-flight requests from recreating removed content.

Your rights. You can download a private JSON export from Profile → Settings → Privacy and save or email it using the iPhone share menu. You can delete your account in the same area, edit your details and withdraw optional permissions. You may also contact studio@byfloq.com about access, correction, erasure, objection, restriction or portability. You may complain to your supervisory authority, including the Spanish AEPD.

Cookies on the new event and invitation pages. These pages do not load advertising or analytics trackers. Essential navigation and security requests remain necessary. Any future non-essential cookies must wait for consent and offer equally visible accept and reject choices.
