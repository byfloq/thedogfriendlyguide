Lokal

Café, art & vintage · Vesterbro

A Vesterbro coffee stop on Sønder Boulevard, with art and vintage design alongside the espresso. An easy pause on a walk through the neighbourhood.

Sønder Boulevard 59, 1720 København V

Dogs are welcome inside.

Get Directions → View on Instagram

Apotek 57

Café & bakery · Nyboder

In the former pharmacy that houses the FRAMA Studio Store, this Nyboder café pairs seasonal plates and pastries with warm timber and shelves of ceramics.

Fredericiagade 57, 1310 København K

Dogs are welcome. Ask the café about seating if you plan to stay.

Photography: FRAMA

La Cabra · Nordhavn

Specialty coffee & roastery · Nordhavn

La Cabra’s harbour-side roastery and café pairs carefully brewed coffee with a brick-lined room. Make it a stop on a walk through Nordhavn’s old port streets.

Århusgade 118X, 2150 Nordhavn

Dogs are welcome at this Nordhavn location. Ask the café about seating.

Photography: La Cabra

Get Directions → View on Instagram

The Roe Bar

Gallery café & brunch · Indre By

The café inside Louise Roe Gallery pairs breakfast and seasonal lunch with coffee and pastries. Its sculptural tables and long counter make it an inviting pause in the city centre.

Vognmagergade 9, 1120 København K

Dogs are welcome inside.

Photography: LOUISE ROE

Get Directions → View on Instagram

Lille Fugl

Bakery café & wine bar · Indre By

Tucked beneath the Gammel Strand art centre, this little café from the Flere Fugle team is a stop for baked goods, breakfast and a glass of wine later in the day.

Læderstræde 9, 1201 København K

Dogs are welcome at Lille Fugl. Ask the café about seating.

Photography: Flere Fugle

Beau Marché

French café & wine bar · Indre By

Past the design shop on Ny Østergade, this French café and wine bar serves classics such as croque monsieur and omelettes. The little courtyard is a quiet place to pause in the city centre.

Ny Østergade 32, 1101 København K

Dogs are welcome. Ask the café whether indoor seating is available.

Photography: VisitCopenhagen

Prolog Coffee · Paper Island

Specialty coffee bar · Christianshavn

Prolog’s Paper Island coffee bar sits on the harbour in Christianshavn. Stop for espresso or filter coffee from the Copenhagen roaster before a walk along the waterfront.

Papirøen 87, 1436 København K

Dogs are welcome inside at Paper Island and at all Prolog coffee bars.

Photography: Prolog Coffee

Amator

Breakfast & lunch restaurant · Østerbro

An intimate Østerbro restaurant where omelettes take centre stage. Long oak tables and a compact open kitchen give breakfast or lunch the feel of a meal at someone’s home.

Nordre Frihavnsgade 7, 2100 København Ø

Dogs are welcome. Ask the restaurant about seating.

Photography: Paolo Galgani

Bottega Barlie

Restaurant & wine bar · Nyboder

A neighbourhood restaurant on Fredericiagade, equally suited to a slow breakfast or a seasonal dinner and a glass of wine. The menu shifts with what is in season.

Fredericiagade 78, 1310 København K

Dogs are welcome. Ask the restaurant about seating.

Photography: Bottega Barlie

Zoku Copenhagen

Apartment hotel · Amager

An Amager aparthotel with compact lofts, full kitchens and rooftop social spaces. A practical base for a longer stay or a weekend with your dog.

Amagerfælledvej 108, 2300 København S

One dog up to 20 kg per loft. A cleaning fee applies. Dogs can join you in most social spaces, with the Living Kitchen restaurant excluded. Hotel pet policy
