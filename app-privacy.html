Browse without an account

You can discover places, read and build plans, and save places without an account. Guest saves and plans stay on that device. When signed in, saved places, saved plans, check-ins and outing memories are synchronised with your account in Supabase. Older device-only saves are imported into an account only if you choose to do so.

Your profile and contributions

If you register or log in, we process your email address, account identifier and any name provided by your sign-in provider to operate and secure your profile. Apple can supply a private relay email if you choose Hide My Email. Sign-in credentials are handled by the sign-in provider and Supabase Authentication; we do not receive your Google or Apple password.

If you choose to contribute, we store your city votes, feature ideas, place requests, reviews, optional photos and check-in information with your account identifier. Check-ins record the chosen place, date and optional dog experience, not a GPS trail. A check-in or passport memory stays private unless you separately choose to share a visit. Approved visit photos, notes and the public name you choose can appear in place reviews, the community and Home stories. Community place requests, their vote counts and names voluntarily supplied with a request or vote may be visible to other users. Profile portraits and your dog's name are kept on your device for the relevant account; they are not automatically used as a public community profile. Private questions and replies are visible to the participants and the Guide's administrators. You can report visits and hide another contributor. A business introduction can include a contact email, website and dog policy you enter.

Location, camera and photos

With your permission, your foreground location is used on your device to calculate approximate distances, display your position during a route and suggest a nearby check-in. You may separately enable background location for optional geofence-based arrival alerts, open saved-place reminders or a reminder after spending time near a place. These features use local entry/exit events; the app does not upload your GPS coordinates or a movement history to our database. If you confirm a check-in, the place and time you choose are saved to your account. You can decline location and still browse or check in manually, and turn optional reminders off in the notification settings or device settings.

Camera and photo access are used when you choose a profile portrait, take or select a visit photo, or create a shareable outing memory. Visit photos are resized and location metadata is removed before submission. Photos submitted with a public visit are stored in Supabase and reviewed before publication; approved photos can be viewed by other app users. Profile portraits and unshared outing images remain on your device. System sharing sends only the content you choose to the destination you select.

Shared lists, invitations and notifications

Shared lists and outings are stored in Supabase. Members can see the list, the chosen display names, additions, votes and any outing date and time. An invitation contains an unguessable token and expires after 30 days. Anyone you give the link to can sign in and choose to join; avoid sharing it publicly if you want a private group. The invitation landing page does not send the token to analytics. You can use the app without joining a shared list.

If you enable remote notifications, we store an Expo push token, platform and chosen language with your account. Expo and Apple's notification service deliver these alerts. Private question and reply text is not included in notification previews. Shared-list activity and place-publication alerts can be controlled by type; location reminders are optional and limited to one per day. You can disable all notifications in device settings.

Services and external links

Supabase hosts our authentication and contribution database in the EU (Frankfurt). Expo delivers app updates. Loading website content, photos, video or maps, signing in and opening external venue links can disclose your IP address and routine technical request information to the relevant service. Maps and walking-direction links may use Apple, Google or OpenStreetMap. Weather requests use a city-centre forecast from MET Norway. Place-name searches are sent through our server to Photon/OpenStreetMap with the selected guide city, not your GPS coordinates. These providers and any external venue site apply their own privacy policies. External services may process data outside the EEA under their applicable safeguards. We use information necessary to run the requested service, protect it and respond to support requests; we do not sell personal data or use the app for advertising tracking.

Why we process data

We use account data and contributions to provide the service you request and respond to your submissions (performance of our service relationship). Optional device permissions and the sharing actions you choose are based on your choices and, where applicable, consent. Security and essential operational logs support our legitimate interests in maintaining a reliable service. We may also retain information where required by law.

Retention and deletion

We retain an account and its linked contributions while the account is in use, unless removed earlier. In Profile, choose Delete account to permanently remove your account and its linked profile, suggestions, votes and guide feedback from the active database. This removes your cloud saves, private outings, questions, contributions, list memberships and votes. Lists you created are removed; remaining list activity no longer identifies your deleted membership. Local portraits, saved collections and passport memories for the deleted account are also cleared on that device. Security logs and provider backups may remain for their normal retention cycles or applicable legal obligations; they are not made available as a restored active account. Deleting the app clears its local app storage; this alone does not delete a registered account.

Your choices and rights

You can withdraw camera, photo and location access in device settings, browse as a guest, sign out, or delete your profile in the app. Contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or to object to processing where those rights apply. You may also contact the Spanish supervisory authority, the AEPD. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Changes

We will update this page if our data practices change and will provide any additional notice or consent required for material changes.
