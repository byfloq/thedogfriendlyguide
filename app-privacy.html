Browse without an account

You can discover places, read plans, create a trip, save places and use Learn without creating an account. Saved places, your trip and learning progress are stored on your device. These are not advertised as a cloud backup or a collaborative editing service.

Your profile and contributions

If you register or log in, we process your email address, account identifier and any name provided by your sign-in provider to operate and secure your profile. Apple can supply a private relay email if you choose Hide My Email. Sign-in credentials are handled by the sign-in provider and Supabase Authentication; we do not receive your Google or Apple password.

If you choose to contribute, we store your city vote, city suggestion, guide usage or completion feedback, rating, note, or place recommendation with your account identifier. A business introduction can additionally include a contact email, website and dog policy that you enter. Place and city suggestions and written feedback are sent privately to the Guide for review. Aggregated city vote counts may be shown to other users; other users are not given a list of individual voters.

Location, camera and photos

With your permission, your foreground location is used on your device to calculate approximate straight-line distances. The app does not send these coordinates to our profile or suggestions database, collect background location, or track your movements. You can decline location and still browse places.

Camera or photo access is used only when you choose to create a story with a photo. Photos are processed on your device. Sharing a story or trip is your choice through the system share sheet; the destination you choose receives the content you share.

Services and external links

Supabase hosts our authentication and contribution database in the EU (Frankfurt). Expo delivers app updates. Loading website content, photos, video or maps, signing in and opening external venue links can disclose your IP address and routine technical request information to the relevant service. An embedded map or a directions link is provided by Google; Google and any external site apply their own privacy policies. External services may process data outside the EEA under their applicable safeguards. We use information necessary to run the requested service, protect it and respond to support requests; we do not sell personal data or use the app for advertising tracking.

Why we process data

We use account data and contributions to provide the service you request and respond to your submissions (performance of our service relationship). Optional device permissions and the sharing actions you choose are based on your choices and, where applicable, consent. Security and essential operational logs support our legitimate interests in maintaining a reliable service. We may also retain information where required by law.

Retention and deletion

We retain an account and its linked contributions while the account is in use, unless removed earlier. In Profile, choose Delete account to permanently remove your account and its linked profile, suggestions, votes and guide feedback from the active database. This also clears the app’s saves, trip and learning progress on that device. Security logs and provider backups may remain for their normal retention cycles or applicable legal obligations; they are not made available as a restored active account. Deleting the app clears its local app storage; this alone does not delete a registered account.

Your choices and rights

You can withdraw camera, photo and location access in device settings, browse as a guest, sign out, or delete your profile in the app. Contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or to object to processing where those rights apply. You may also contact the Spanish supervisory authority, the AEPD. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Changes

We will update this page if our data practices change and will provide any additional notice or consent required for material changes.
