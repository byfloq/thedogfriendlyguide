Privacy notice

The Dog Friendly Guide is operated by SECOND NATURE SL, NIF B-67432732. Commercial and correspondence contact: C. Aribau, 168, 1.º 1.ª, 08036 Barcelona, Spain. Privacy enquiries: studio@byfloq.com. Last updated: 19 November 2026.

We use account details, your human and dog profile, home city, preferences and the content you choose to provide to operate the Guide, personalise recommendations and manage connections and reservations. You can browse places as a guest. Participation, cloud saves, messages, community contributions and reservations require an account. We store account identifiers and your email for authentication; sign-in providers process their own authentication data.

Moments and photos. Your private Pack consists of people you choose to connect with; both people agree to connect. Your moments are visible to that Pack. If “My moments on place pages” is enabled, the team can review and publish your contribution on a place page under your chosen public name. You can turn this off in Privacy and delete your own moments. We remove location metadata from uploaded photos. Only share photos you have permission to share. A separate choice controls whether an event photo can be shown on its club page.

Shared profiles and community. Your profile brings together you and your dog, using separate names and portraits. Your exact dog birth date remains private; shared introductions can include derived age, breed, size, traits, city, biography and the social handles you add. Public discoverability, profile links and use of your human portrait follow your privacy choices; permission to show the human portrait is separate. Public questions, replies, clubs, events and other content you deliberately publish can be seen by their intended audience. Private messages and private Pack content remain restricted to their participants. A profile link does not grant access to private messages or moments. You can report content and block users. Changing the city you browse does not change your profile home city.

Events. We store the event, your dog’s name, reservation status, ticket code, attendance and your photo choice. The host sees what is needed to welcome and check in participants, including an indication if you do not want to appear in photographs. You can change the default photo choice in Privacy and the choice for each reservation before confirming. After attending, you can choose whether other attendees can find you and invite you to connect. Declined requests remain private.

Reservations in this version are free. Payments and Apple Pay are disabled; we do not collect card details or send payment data to Stripe in this version. If paid events are introduced, their price and applicable terms will be shown before booking and this notice will be updated.

Location and notifications. Location and notification permissions are optional and controlled by the operating system. Your chosen city and area support nearby recommendations. If you allow location, precise coordinates may be processed to show your position and nearby places. Places you voluntarily mark as visited, private places you save and location details you choose to include in a plan may identify a precise location and are stored for those features. We remove photo location metadata during upload; this does not make every location-related feature anonymous. Embedded Google Maps processes connection and device information and may receive location coordinates when you use location features. Push delivery uses an installation token associated with your account. You can change app notification preferences and system permissions in Settings.

Analytics and diagnostics. Optional product analytics and optional usage recordings are separate choices, both off by default. Declining them does not prevent using the app. If enabled, PostHog processes usage events and a random identifier stored on your device and kept separate for each account or guest session in its European region. These are pseudonymous data, rather than a guarantee of anonymous data. Optional recordings are limited to Explore, with input fields and images masked; they stop on private screens, in messages and in the background. You can withdraw consent in Privacy to stop future collection. We record your consent choice, notice version and the time and source of that choice. Essential diagnostics use Sentry and service security logs to investigate errors and maintain the app. Technical reports can contain device or installation identifiers, operating-system and app details, and crash or diagnostic information. The app limits its diagnostic payloads; provider-side processing and retention also apply.

Ask the Guide and Coco (AI). You can ask Coco, our AI guide, or send a question to the human Guide team. Before your first use of Coco, we ask permission to send your question, earlier Coco questions and answers, and relevant public Guide information to OpenAI to prepare an answer. Your profile, photos, precise location and questions addressed to the human team are not automatically included. OpenAI may retain submitted content for security and abuse prevention under its API data controls; this is separate from your history in the Guide.

Ask history and access. Signed-in questions, answers and related human replies are stored privately until account deletion; the app shows the latest 45 questions. Questions deliberately sent to the human team are available only to assigned, authorized Guide staff. Guest questions and answers are deleted by an hourly cleanup after 24 hours, normally within about 25 hours. Pseudonymous network hashes help limit guest abuse and are not included in the AI context. Your private account export includes your own Ask questions, answers and related replies. Account deletion removes these records.

Providers and purposes. Supabase supplies authentication, databases and media storage. Expo and the operating-system push services deliver notifications. PostHog processes optional analytics and masked recordings. Sentry processes error diagnostics. Google Maps supplies embedded maps. Network requests can expose IP addresses and technical connection information to the service handling them. We use providers to deliver requested functionality, personalise the service, support users and maintain security, with applicable safeguards for international transfers. We do not sell personal data or use our own advertising networks. Provider privacy information is available from Supabase, Expo, PostHog, Sentry and Google.

Legal bases and retention. We process account and reservation data to provide the service you request. Optional public sharing, location, notifications and analytics follow your choices and permissions; security and abuse prevention support our legitimate interests. We retain account content while it is needed to operate your active account. Removing a post, comment or contribution may first withdraw it from view without immediately erasing every stored copy. Some content or records may remain where necessary to investigate complaints, prevent abuse, maintain security or meet legal obligations. Account deletion starts the removal of private content, connections, future reservations and device tokens; past tickets are anonymised. Removal can require retries. Limited technical deletion records prevent late requests from restoring removed account data. You can contact us about erasure and applicable retention criteria.

Your rights. You can download a private JSON export from Profile → Settings → Privacy and save or email it using the iPhone share menu. You can delete your account in the same area, edit your details and withdraw optional permissions. You may also contact studio@byfloq.com about access, correction, erasure, objection, restriction or portability. You may complain to your supervisory authority, including the Spanish AEPD.

Cookies on the new event and invitation pages. These pages do not load advertising or analytics trackers. Essential navigation and security requests remain necessary. Any future non-essential cookies must wait for consent and offer equally visible accept and reject choices.
